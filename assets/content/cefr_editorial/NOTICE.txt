LexiQuest curated meanings and bilingual examples — A1–C2 editorial revision 2
Created 2026-09-12 by AI-assisted editorial work for LexiQuest.

Coverage: one primary sense and one original English example with a Thai
translation for each of the 5,500 headwords in the current inventory:
5,000 A1–C1 core words and a separate 500-word C2 supplement.
Examples were individually authored, reread and independently cross-reviewed
by AI. They were not copied from dictionary examples. This is not human approval,
CEFR certification, validation of each sense's level, or evidence of learning efficacy.

The original CEFR-J headwords/levels and LEXiTRON translations remain unchanged
in the base catalog. Their attribution and full applicable licenses remain in
cefr_starter/NOTICE.txt, LICENSE.txt and LICENSE-th.txt. The editorial glosses
clarify/select equivalent source senses or supply independently authored senses
where the original glossary is unsuitable; sourceMeaningIndex records equivalence.
An optional explicit POS override applies only to the curated example, never to
the original catalog or previously saved user vocabulary.

The inherited headword labels are approximate and do not mean every specialized
sense is independently rated at that level. All 10,793 original glossary senses
have an AI editorial inclusion/exclusion decision with reasons for exclusions.
Excluded original glosses remain in the immutable source catalog, but are not
offered as new import choices. Existing saved words retain their original data.
No human reviewer or external certification is claimed. Example content is
presentation-only, not verified exercise evidence.
