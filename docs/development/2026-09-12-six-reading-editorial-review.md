# Six-reading starter editorial review

Scope agreed on 2026-09-12: retain six original passages, one per approximate
A1–C2 level, with one reflection question each. This is an assistant editorial
review, not independent teacher review, a CEFR certification or a full course.
The on-screen provisional-level notice remains on every reader.

| Level / title | Language and difficulty | Reflection answer supported by text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 / A book for May | Concrete school objects, short present-tense clauses; `put ... away` and `together` may need support. | A book and a pencil are in May’s bag. |
| A2 / A change of plan | Past-tense sequence, common destinations and a simple reason for changing plans. | It started raining, so they went to the library instead. |
| B1 / The shared garden | Connected narrative, past habit, shared responsibility and a relative clause. Replaced the unnatural `space ... used to collect rubbish` with `used to be full of rubbish`; content revision r2. | They made a watering schedule and shared responsibility. |
| B2 / Repair before replacement | Concrete community example with an abstract argument, qualifications and cause/effect. Replaced unnecessary `ephemeral` with `initial enthusiasm may fade`; retained contextual `resilient`; content revision r2. | Shared responsibilities, clear roles, useful skills, regular training and a realistic plan help the project last. |
| C1 / Measuring a useful service | Abstract evaluation, alternative interpretations, qualification and participation bias; coherent and natural. | A short visit could indicate efficient access or frustration; visit counts alone cannot distinguish these. |
| C2 / The convenience of certainty | Dense abstract argument, qualified claims and revision of conclusions; coherent and natural. | Separate established knowledge from plausible claims, recommend action provisionally, and state what evidence would justify revision. |

Each question can be answered from its passage; none requires external facts.
These are reflection prompts, without graded responses or automatic proof of
comprehension. Completing an independent reading must not create quiz attempts,
learning time, rewards or research evidence. Vocabulary-linked reading follows
its existing activity evidence rules.

Six short texts cannot represent the breadth of a level, particularly C1/C2.
The progression is an editorial estimate based on vocabulary, syntax and
argument complexity, not a vocabulary-frequency audit or validated proficiency
instrument. Expansion and additional questions are outside this agreed scope.

Acceptance evidence is recorded in the 2026-09-12 device checkpoint: all six
original readers were opened on vivo, and revised content has focused catalog,
route and narrow-screen tests. Physical checks of the revised preview are
reported separately from those earlier R4 checks.
