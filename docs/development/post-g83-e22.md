- Planning hub opens a real plan editor, current goal selector, due-first proposal, item/count/time diff, reject/accept/replan, and links back to canonical home and personal sets. Goal deadline/timezone come from the current goal. No goal uses the runtime learning timezone.
- Policy `due-first-minute-estimate-v1`: one minute per word is an explicit planning estimate, not a measured completion time. Capacity is 0–1440 minutes. Canonical SRS due items are ordered by due time then word ID; new organization references come from latest unarchived F01 sets, deduplicated at historical word identity and excluding every existing SRS word. No second scheduler or scored admission is created. F01/canonical launch continues to own content and scoring admission.
- Unallocated due work stays in carry-over. Replanning reads current SRS rather than accumulating stale previous carry-over. Calendar dates use IANA timezone/DST; elapsed full local dates since the last accepted plan are planning metadata, not proof of days without learning. Past goal deadlines do not erase progress.
- Accepted rows are immutable; owner is the enclosing key so guest transfer preserves payload hashes. Operation ID is immutable revision identity; revision number is the active planning lineage sequence. A single owner pointer selects the active accepted row. Proposal is read-only; insert and pointer CAS are atomic. Exact lost-ACK replay precedes current source admission and never rewinds a newer active pointer. Changed-payload collisions and stale revisions/sources fail closed.
- Schema 30 adds `study_plan_revisions` and `active_plan_pointers`. Their v30 migration unit is atomic and runs after historical migrations, which retain their own FK/transaction boundaries. SQL rejects payload update and INSERT OR REPLACE while permitting canonical owner remapping and erasure.
- Export/deletion manifest includes both tables. Guest merge keeps the target active plan, preserves both histories, reconciles exact duplicate operations, and rejects conflicting identities transactionally. Scoped plan-history backup/restore is owner-bound and cannot replace a live pointer with older history. It does not claim to restore a redacted lifecycle export or the whole database.
- Owner generation plus canonical lease fences all application operations. Accept also rechecks the destination/feature and rolls back on retirement. Idle UI clears private state on durable epoch change. Organization writes do not create attempts, SRS changes, rewards, research rows or remote outbox operations.
