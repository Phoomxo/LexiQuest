## Source facts and limits

- Field defaults hide studyPlanning, researchAssessment, dailyContinuity,
  offlineContent and adventureMotivation. Camera, speech and AI are limited.
  These are source defaults, not observed deployment/runtime override values.
- `LEXIQUEST_LEARNING_PREVIEW` defaults false. Bootstrap enables Matching only
  for learning preview; Scratchpad is implemented-off. Focus timer is internal
  under preview and implemented-off otherwise. G0.8/P7.5 own edition decisions.
- Adventure's production entry is `home/learn/today-experience`; Ghost is
  `drawer/learning/ghost-duel`. Entry presence does not establish journey success.
- Cloud sync defaults true. This package neither activates research nor changes
  cloud, model, paid services or device configuration.
- Starter catalog is `cefr-starter-3000-r1`, 3,000 entries, marked
  `source-joined-not-independently-reviewed`. Content-addressed editorial assets
  are separately pinned. Their presence does not establish publication/readiness
  for every activity. Canonical pack/word revisions and checksums are resolved
  per session; no live database/session revision was fabricated here.
- MG-01 remains block spelling using wordScramble, including tap/drag/return tile
  semantics. MG-03 selected cloze and MG-04 typed cloze remain distinct. MG-10
  requires authored Q&A; exposure-only reading cannot close it. Self-rating,
  scratchpad and supplemental speech cannot be promoted to scored evidence.

inspection. [Verifier](evidence/G0.5/verify_traceability.py) checks complete
identities, eight domains, valid owners, 17 route-free consumer boundaries,
source/default route declarations, content/authority links, hashes and status
preservation. It also checks that application, test, asset and verifier-tooling
inputs remain unchanged from the accepted source. No Flutter, backend, Android,
GPU or release suite ran. Existing G0.2/G0.4 results remain prior evidence only.

All 71 needs-verification and three delivery-decision-pending statuses remain.
No row becomes verified-existing. Physical audio, media/model readiness, actual
deployment config, published question sets and runtime journeys remain assigned
to their owner packages. G0.3 D01–D12 and mandatory missing Economy/Integration
gate obligations from G0.4 are preserved. G0 phase is still in progress.

Owner-path expansion is limited to the master ledger, this report, checkpoint
and reproducible documentation inspection scripts/evidence. Runtime catalog,
mode registry, bootstrap, schema, dependencies and generator remain unchanged.
